## 🌍 Overview

**LuxeWay 2.0** is a luxury travel discovery platform designed around elegant digital experiences, curated destinations, premium accommodation, and personalized journey planning.

The platform's vision is to bring multiple luxury travel categories into one cohesive experience, helping travelers explore possibilities and plan exceptional journeys.

### The LuxeWay experience

- 🏨 **Luxury stays:** Hotels, private villas, resorts, and boutique properties.
- ✈️ **Private aviation:** Explore private jet and premium air-transfer options.
- 🛥️ **Yacht charters:** Discover exclusive marine experiences.
- 🚘 **Luxury transportation:** Premium cars and chauffeur services.
- 🌍 **Curated destinations:** Explore destinations through themed collections.
- 🍽️ **Fine dining:** Discover culinary experiences and premium restaurants.
